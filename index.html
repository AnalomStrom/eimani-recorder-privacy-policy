01 / OverviewAbout this policy

This Privacy Policy covers Eimani Recorder, an Android screen-recording app developed by Analom Studio, with application ID com.analom.eimanirecorder.

The app processes recordings and related information on your device. Its recording features do not implement a recording-upload service or a developer-operated cloud library. Google Play services, Android backups, services you choose for sharing, and messages you independently send to support have separate data flows described below.

Eimani Recorder does not provide its own account-registration or sign-in system. Any Google Play account used for Google Play services is managed by Google.

02 / CaptureScreen and audio recording

When you start a recording and approve Android's screen-capture prompt, the app captures the screen content Android makes available to that session. Depending on the selected audio mode and permissions, it can also capture microphone audio, permitted internal playback audio, both sources, or no audio.

Screen content can contain messages, account details, notifications, images and other personal information displayed by other apps. Microphone recordings can include your voice, nearby conversations and background sounds. Choose what to record and obtain any permission needed from other people.

Internal audio capture requires Android 10 or later and is subject to Android and the source app's capture restrictions. Recording can continue while you use other apps, with a foreground recording service and available status controls. You can stop it using the app or available recording controls.

Recordings are saved as MP4 video in the device's shared Movies/EimaniRecorder location, through Android MediaStore on newer devices or shared filesystem storage on older supported devices. The recording code does not automatically upload this content to Analom Studio.

05 / External servicesInternet access, Google Play and purchases

The release app uses Google Play Billing Library 9.1.0. It connects to Google Play to query product information and existing purchase status, including on startup and when the app resumes. Network activity is therefore not limited to pressing a purchase button.

Purchase handling can process product identifiers, purchase tokens, purchase state, acknowledgement status and derived entitlement information. The app's purchase snapshots and entitlement state are held in memory for purchase and access checks; the SDK's diagnostic storage is described below. The app does not present a form for card numbers or payment credentials.

Live checkout is unavailable in the reviewed configuration because purchase verification is not configured. No developer-operated purchase-verification endpoint is implemented. If that changes, the policy must reflect the new data flow. Debug builds can instead store a simulated plan and expiry locally; simulation does not initiate a Google Play payment.

A build fingerprint describes a software build, not a fingerprint scan. These findings do not establish whether other identifiers or data appear elsewhere in the complete SDK payload.

The logging transport can queue encoded events and metadata in an on-device SQLite database before sending them to a configured Google HTTPS logging endpoint. Local queue storage and cleanup are separate from Google's storage after receipt. The app does not expose a separate billing-diagnostics opt-out in the reviewed source.

The billing dependencies include Google data-transport and Firebase encoding utilities. Encoding utilities and a Google logging endpoint do not establish a Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics integration. Transitive Play services location components are present, but no app-owned location API calls or location permissions were found.

No claim is made that billing diagnostics are anonymous, processed only in memory, or deleted by Google after a fixed period.

Google handles its services under the Google Privacy Policy. Android and Google Play may also process app-quality information, such as crashes and performance, according to your device and usage-diagnostics settings. See Android vitals.

06 / Your choiceSharing and opening recordings

When you select a share or external-player action, the app uses Android's sharing or viewing interface and grants the selected app access to the chosen recording. This can disclose the video, its audio and accompanying file information to that app or its recipients.

A recipient service can upload, retain or further share the content under its own rules. Shared recordings may also be accessible to gallery or media apps under Android's storage controls. Deleting a local recording does not remove copies already received by others.

07 / System servicesBackups and device transfers

Android backup is enabled, and the current backup rules do not expressly exclude app data. Eligible preferences, the local history database, SDK databases and other eligible app-private files may therefore be included in Android cloud backup or device transfer, depending on your device, provider and settings. A backup or restore was not observed during this source review.

Videos in shared storage may separately be copied by gallery/cloud-backup apps you enable. Those copies are controlled by the relevant service. Local storage does not guarantee that information stays only on the original device. See Android backup information.

08 / Data lifecycleRetention and deletion

  • Recordings: no time-based recording-deletion schedule is configured. Use the app's Gallery delete action, or a device gallery/file manager, to remove recordings. The app attempts to remove the selected file or media item before deleting its local history entry. If deletion fails, the history entry is retained.
  • Settings and history: these persist in app-private storage. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes that local app data under Android's behavior. Shared videos can remain and should be checked separately.
  • Backups and recipient copies: manage deletion through the relevant backup service, receiving app or recipient. Analom Studio has no implemented remote-delete feature for those copies.
  • Google Play records: Google controls its purchase and service records. Clearing app data or uninstalling does not itself delete Google's records or cancel a subscription.
  • Logs and temporary data: the billing transport includes local event-queue cleanup and retry logic. When cleanup occurs depends on the SDK's execution and device conditions; this is not a guaranteed deletion deadline. Removing local queue dataচেতন does not remove events already received by Google. Google's server-side SDK-log retention period has not been verified. Android governs its own diagnostic logs.

There is no Eimani Recorder account to delete. Support correspondence is addressed separately below.

09 / ProtectionSecurity and its limits

Settings and history use Android app-private storage. The recording service and file provider are not exported for unrestricted external access. User-initiated sharing grants read access to the selected recording through Android's URI-permission mechanism.

The app does not implement an additional encryption or password-protection layer for saved videos or its local history database. Videos are stored in shared media storage. Device access controls, operating-system protections, backups and other apps' granted access affect their security.

The examined billing logging transport is configured with a Google HTTPS endpoint. This configuration check does not establish that every possible SDK or system-service data flow has been verified as encrypted in transit.

No storage or communication system can be guaranteed completely secure. Avoid recording sensitive screens or conversations unnecessarily, protect access to your device, and review a recipient before sharing.

10 / Separate correspondenceSupport emails and privacy requests

If you independently email analomstudio@gmail.com, your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send are delivered to the support mailbox. This is separate from data accessed or stored by the Android app; the app does not automatically send support emails or attach recordings.

Analom Studio has not yet established a retention/deletion policy for support emails and attachments. The Android source does not verify how the mailbox is accessed, organized, forwarded, backed up or cleared. No fixed storage period, automatic-deletion deadline or guaranteed deletion outcome is stated here. You can write to the same address with privacy questions or an access, correction or deletion request where applicable; the operational handling of those requests still requires confirmation.

Only include information needed to explain your issue. Do not send passwords, payment credentials or sensitive recordings unnecessarily. Email delivery and storage also involve your email provider and the receiving mailbox's provider.

11 / Intended audienceChildren's privacy

Eimani Recorder is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not provide its own registration or date-of-birth collection feature.

If you are a parent or guardian with concerns about a child's information in support correspondence, contact Analom Studio. Information within a locally stored recording must be managed on the device and, if shared, with the relevant recipient. Recording other people should respect their privacy and applicable requirements.
